=================================================================
2. USER FLOW IMPLEMENTATION
=================================================================

STEP 1: Browse Courses (/courses)
  - Student views grid of available published courses
  - Click on any course card

STEP 2: Course Overview (/course-overview/:id)
  - View full course details:
    * Course title, description, category, level
    * Teacher information
    * Course curriculum (modules and topics)
    * Learning outcomes
    * Number of enrolled students
  - Click "Enroll Now" button

STEP 3: Course Player (/course/:title/play)
  - Access course content and lessons
  - Start learning
